Fair and Explainable AI & Data Science (FXAIDS) Lab

Advancing algorithmic fairness, explainable artificial intelligence (XAI), social network analysis, applied machine learning, and computational social science for transparent, high-stakes algorithmic decision-making. Our research group bridges the Department of Management Studies (DoMS) and the Mehta Family School of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (MFSDS&AI) at IIT Roorkee.
